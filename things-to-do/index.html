Kojohama & Shiraoi area guide

Slow days between sea, onsen and living culture.

Start close to the cabins: walk the coast, taste Kojohama seafood and end the day in a local onsen. Then use Ocean Stay as a quiet base for Upopoy, Noboribetsu and the wider Shiraoi area.

Stay local

A day in Kojohama

Coast, shrine and lighthouse

Take a gentle morning walk toward Ayoro Beach and the lighthouse viewpoints. The coast is especially atmospheric at sunrise, but conditions can be windy or icy in winter.

Seafood and tarako

Kojohama is known for tarako, or salted cod roe. Look for local fish shops and lunch at Tatchan Shokudo; always confirm current hours and closing days.

Local onsen

Ayoro Onsen and Kojohama Onsen Hotel offer nearby bathing options. Day-bathing hours and temporary closures can change, so check on the day.

Gelato and ice cream

Yu Gelato sits right in Kojohama, on the grounds of the 24-hour Hana no Yu Onsen — handmade gelato in rotating local flavours such as corn, red shiso, pomegranate and asparagus, good after a soak or a coastal walk. In central Shiraoi, near Shiraoi Station, Gelateria Mina Pieno turns Hokkaido milk into around a dozen gelato flavours alongside soft serve and parfaits; it is run by the community welfare organisation Miraie. Hours and closures vary at both, so check before you go.

Antiques in Takeura

Antique Shop Route 36 (Shiraoi branch) sits about 4 minutes by car from the cabins, at 118-174 Takeura. It is packed with Meiji- and Taisho-era furniture, old tableware and curiosities, and is a good rainy-day browse for anyone who likes old things. Open Thursday to Sunday, 10:00–16:00 (closed Monday to Wednesday); hours can change, so check the map listing before you go.

An evening in Shiraoi: bar hopping

The local Shiraoi Masters Club publishes a Pub & Bar Hopping Guide to three small bars, all a short walk from JR Shiraoi Station. J's BAR (2F Best Denki building, 3-2-1 Omachi; tel. 0144-84-8677; 19:00–24:00, closed Tuesdays and the 1st and 3rd Mondays) is a relaxed, shoes-off bar with darts. coffee&whisky kaiser (3-2-41 Omachi; tel. 0144-82-2300; 19:30–24:00, closed Sundays and Mondays) is an eight-seat counter serving house-roasted coffee, Japanese whisky and coffee cocktails. Public House W (3-9-8 Omachi; tel. 0144-82-5500; 19:00–24:00, closed Sundays) has a big bottle collection and karaoke. Most charge a cover of ¥550–¥1,000. Please don't drive after drinking: take the JR train between Kojohama and Shiraoi stations, or a taxi (Shiraoi Kotsu, 0144-82-2244). Hours can change, so check before you go.

Event: Ainu Art Show 2026 at Upopoy, 11–12 October

Upopoy, about 25 minutes from the cabins, hosts the Ainu Art Show on Sunday 11 and Monday 12 October 2026 (a national holiday). Around 18 Ainu artists and craftspeople exhibit and sell their work from 10:00 to 17:00 in the Experience and Learning Hall, joined by two Hopi silversmiths from the United States as special guests. Artist and musician OKI premieres his new documentary, Big Ekashi of Poroto (about 30 minutes, four screenings a day in the multipurpose hall), with a short stage greeting before the first screening each day and a talk from 14:30 to 15:00 (80 places a day). There are also artist talks, hands-on workshops and Ainu food tastings (¥1,000 each; numbered tickets are handed out on the day before each session). The events are inside Upopoy, so you need a park ticket: adults ¥1,200, high-school students ¥600, free for junior-high age and under, with a half-price ticket for entry from 14:00. Details can change; check the Upopoy website or call 0144-82-3914.

From our guest guide

Around the cabins: 65 places to eat, soak, shop and explore

This is the same list our guests use in the in-cabin guide, where each place also has opening hours and a map link. Times are approximate drives from the cabins. Hours and prices change, so check before a special trip.

Grill your finds: BBQ on request

Picked up scallops, dried fish or shiitake along the way? A Coleman outdoor BBQ grill is available on request. Just ask us before your stay. The usage fee is ¥2,000, which covers cleaning by our team afterwards.

Eat and drink

  • Kojohama Cafe1 min

    Sea terrace and Japanese-style tarako pasta. The closest sit-down meal to the cabins (about 4 minutes on foot).

  • Ayoro Onsen cafeteria3 min

    Self-service hall after a bath. Tarako fried rice, and a meal set that includes the soak.

  • Shokujidokoro Ikeda3 min

    Local diner on Route 36 with fresh local seafood; the sashimi set is the favourite. Soba and ramen too.

  • Yu Gelato3 min

    Handmade gelato at Hana no Yu, made with Noboribetsu milk and seasonal local fruit and vegetables.

  • Tacchan Shokudo4 min

    A seafood company's cafeteria: donburi, salt ramen and the day's fish. The village lunch locals actually use.

  • Akebono4 min

    Orange-sign ramen on the station road: miso, salt, soy and miso-curry, with gyoza wrapped after you order. One of the few village dinners.

  • Tarakoya Kojohama4 min

    House-cured Kojohama tarako on rice. The village is famous for salted cod roe, and this is the specialist.

  • Yamamoto Trout Farm5 min

    Catch your own trout in spring-fed ponds; they cook it as sashimi, grilled or fried. Fun with children.

  • Hikaru Umi5 min

    Seafood restaurant with Pacific views: kaisen-don, tendon and hokki (surf clam) curry. Cards accepted.

  • Kanitaro5 min

    A retro local legend with one dish: a ¥500 crab-rice set, about 20 a day. Queue before opening.

  • Sobakiri Yamada6 min

    Stone-ground, hand-cut soba in an old farmhouse with tatami seating. Try the tenzaru and the soba pudding.

  • Tonton diner6 min

    Home-style set meals and curry in big portions. One of the few places open for dinner.

  • Ramen Sakuraya7 min

    Morning ramen from 9:00: miso, tonkotsu and flying-fish broth. A Takeura habit on the way to Shiraoi.

  • Kita no Lamp Tei7 min

    Charcoal grill by the Pacific: Shiraoi beef, lamb and scallops, with a sea-view deck. The long lunch in Takeura.

  • Kani-o8 min

    Pick a live crab from the tank and they boil it in front of you. Crab-rice set if you don't want a whole crab.

  • Nachu no Mori Smile cafeteria8 min

    Cooking with Lake Kuttara spring water: pizza, plates and a kids' corner. Easy with children.

  • Distill Cafe8 min

    Coffee and light plates in the old village school, with spring-water drinks and a terrace on fine days.

  • Restaurant Cowbell14 min

    Log-house yakiniku run by a Shiraoi butcher. Certified Shiraoi beef over charcoal.

  • Iwasaki Omotenashi-tei22 min

    Shiraoi beef from their own farm as yakiniku, sukiyaki or shabu-shabu. The special-night dinner.

  • Aji no Daio honke28 min

    The bowl that started Tomakomai curry ramen, on Route 36 toward the airport.

  • Date Okina38 min

    Hand-cut ni-hachi soba, worth the drive to Date for a quiet, serious bowl.

Onsen and day baths

  • Ayoro Onsen3 min

    Local day bath with skin-soft sodium-chloride water and an outdoor tub. The most affordable soak nearby.

  • Hotel Izumi (day bath)3 min

    Pure source-fed spring with no heating, water or recirculation added. Outdoor bath over the Pacific, plus a sauna.

  • Hana no Yu Onsen3 min

    Shiraoi's only 24-hour hot spring, source-fed, with private family baths. Good for a late-night or sunrise soak.

  • Hamachidori private baths3 min

    Five private source-fed baths rented by the room, for couples and families who prefer not to share.

  • Kojohama Onsen Hotel4 min

    Free-flowing 'beauty spring', outdoor bath and an auto-löyly sauna. The full hotel soak in the village.

  • Hotel Ocean (day bath)5 min

    Ten kinds of bath, several outdoors, hot and cold saunas and sea views. Good value.

  • Yumoto Hokuyo (day bath)5 min

    Showa-era onsen inn with a huge outdoor bath, open from 5 a.m. for a dawn soak.

Sights and day trips

  • Sunrise shrine3 min

    A small shrine above the shore. New Year's sunrise is famous, and any clear dawn through the torii is worth the early walk.

  • Ayoro Beach & lighthouse5 min

    Black sand, tetrapods and a working lighthouse. An easy walk after morning coffee.

  • Toy & Art Museum (Koshu Kitano)7 min

    A private collection of 10,000+ items: Showa tin toys, Godzilla and Ultraman vinyl, robots and masks, with antiques downstairs.

  • Nachu no Mori8 min

    Gardens, a skincare workshop, the old village school and a cafe, in the same village as the cabins.

  • Noboribetsu Date Jidaimura12 min

    An Edo-period town you can walk through: ninja house, oiran show and costumes. Easy with children.

  • Lake Kuttara15 min

    A perfectly round caldera lake in the hills above Kojohama, once rated Japan's clearest. Viewing deck and silent forest.

  • Noboribetsu Marine Park NIXE15 min

    Castle-shaped aquarium with penguins, dolphin shows and a tunnel of fish. Easy with children.

  • Noboribetsu Jigokudani (Hell Valley)16 min

    A steaming volcanic valley you can walk, free and always open. The classic Hokkaido onsen landscape.

  • Upopoy National Ainu Museum and Park20–25 min

    The national centre for Ainu culture: museum, kotan village, dance and food.

  • Lake Poroto20–25 min

    Still water beside Upopoy for a short walk or a coffee after the museum. Swans in winter.

  • Sendai-han Shiraoi Jinya20 min

    An 1856 Sendai-clan garrison site, with a samurai-house-style museum and earthworks. A quieter hour in Shiraoi.

  • Muroran factory night view & Hakucho Bridge28 min

    Steel-town lights on the water and a suspension bridge lit gold. A night drive rather than a ticketed sight.

  • Lake Utonai28 min

    Ramsar wetland on the way to the airport, with swans, geese and a free visitor centre.

  • Kitakogane Shell Mound30 min

    UNESCO World Heritage Jomon site with pit dwellings and a huge shell mound. Free; open April to November.

  • Cape Chikyu32 min

    A white lighthouse on a 130 m cliff, with a Pacific horizon that seems to curve. Muroran's southern cape.

  • Usu Zenkoji40 min

    One of the three official Edo-period temples of Ezo, with cherry blossom, hydrangeas and a quiet hall.

Local food, crafts and souvenirs

  • Tarako vending machine3 min

    One of the few tarako vending machines in Japan. A photo stop as much as a snack.

  • Tairyobanya Kojohama3 min

    Fill a jar with Kojohama tarako yourself. The souvenir stop next to Seicomart.

  • Takemaru Shibuya Suisan (tarako)3 min

    Award-winning tarako and mentaiko maker with a factory shop. Cards accepted; ships home.

  • Sakanaya Honpo (dried fish)3 min

    Traditional fish dried in sun and sea breeze, made on site. Perfect on the cabin BBQ grill (available on request).

  • Kojohama Kanbara4 min

    Another village tarako maker, quieter than Tairyobanya.

  • Kikyohara Farm (shiitake)5 min

    Award-winning log-grown shiitake. Pick your own in season, or buy thick caps picked that morning for the cabin kitchen or BBQ.

  • Antique Shop Route 365 min

    A roadside barn of Showa-retro goods, old tools, ceramics, kimono and carved wooden bears. Made for browsing.

  • Hashimoto Pottery Gallery5 min

    Handmade pottery by local makers, plus house-roasted coffee beans to take home.

  • Marugo weekend seafood stall6 min

    Weekend-only stall on Route 36 with good-value crab, scallops and clams. Cash only.

  • Malcove bakery6 min

    Hidden hillside bakery with natural-yeast bread from a stone oven. Locals queue, so go early.

  • Nachu no Mori shop8 min

    Skincare made with Lake Kuttara water, calendula and hydrosols. A gentler souvenir than tarako.

  • Poroto Mintara20 min

    Shiraoi tourist information, Ainu-pattern goods, local food and soft serve next to the station.

  • Plat Minato Market35 min

    Tomakomai's port fish market: hokki clams, donburi counters and fruit stalls. Eat there, then pack seafood for the cabin fridge.

Everyday shopping and essentials

  • Seicomart Kojohama3 min

    The village convenience store: hot food, rice balls, milk, detergent and ice. The closest shop.

  • Seicomart Yahaba5 min

    The second village Seicomart, toward Noboribetsu.

  • FamilyMart Takeura7 min

    Open 24 hours, with an ATM.

  • JCHO Noboribetsu Hospital8 min

    The nearest hospital. Call ahead; in an emergency dial 119.

  • Coop Paseo Shiraoi18 min

    The full supermarket: produce, meat, sake and a drugstore counter. Stock up here if you are cooking for several nights.

  • Lucky Mart Shiraoi18 min

    A smaller supermarket near Shiraoi station.

  • Tsuruha Drug Shiraoi19 min

    Pharmacy, cosmetics and baby goods.

  • Aeon Mall Tomakomai36 min

    The big mall: groceries, shops, food court and cinema. Handy for a rainy afternoon.
